
# 🍱 BhojanBond

## Problem Statement
Food waste and hunger exist simultaneously. BhojanBond helps connect surplus food donors with NGOs and volunteers.

## Solution
BhojanBond is an AI-powered food redistribution platform that uses AI and automation to reduce food waste.

## Features
- Food Donation Registration
- AI Food Safety Assistant
- NGO & Volunteer Dashboard
- Smart Matching
- Email Notifications

## Technology
- Lyzr AI
- n8n
- Firebase
- Flutter (Planned)
